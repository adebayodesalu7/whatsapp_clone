TITAN CHATAPP - COMPLETE FEATURE OUTLINE

1. CORE COMMUNICATION SUITE
---------------------------
* Real-Time Messaging: Instant text messaging with delivery and read receipts (single and double ticks).
* Multimedia Sharing: Send and receive high-quality images, videos, and documents (PDFs, Docs).
* Voice Notes: Integrated audio recorder with waveform visualization.
* Voice & Video Call Logs: Dashboard to track incoming, outgoing, and missed calls.
* Group Conversations: Create and manage groups with "Active" status indicators for members.
* Broadcast Channels: Follow favorite creators and businesses for one-way updates.
* Contact Management: Sync with phone contacts to find friends automatically.

2. TITAN AI ECOSYSTEM
---------------------
* AI Image-to-Video: Transform static images into dynamic, zooming AI video previews in-chat.
* AI Business Bot: Automated assistant for customer inquiries and away-mode auto-replies.
* Smart AI Search: Contextual search that understands user intent.
* AI Auto-Tagging: Detects objects in marketplace photos (e.g., "Car", "Electronics") automatically.
* AI Content Moderation: Built-in safety shield (AWS Rekognition) to block inappropriate content.
* Google Translation: Auto-translate chat messages and tags into multiple languages.

3. MARKETPLACE & BUSINESS TOOLS
-------------------------------
* Peer-to-Peer Commerce: List products for sale with multi-photo support and categories.
* Secure Titan Escrow: Built-in safety layer holding payments until buyer confirmation.
* Smart Pricing: Professional currency formatting (e.g., N1,200,000) with real-time input masking.
* AR Product Preview: "View in your Room" feature to visualize products using Augmented Reality.
* Seller Dashboard: Manage listings with Edit and Delete functionality for owners.
* Invoicing System: Generate and send professional invoices directly within chat threads.
* Business Profiling: Specialized tools for business accounts and catalogs.

4. FINTECH & SOCIAL SAVINGS
---------------------------
* Integrated Wallet: Manage funds, track transaction history, and perform wallet-to-wallet transfers.
* Ajo Savings Groups: Digital rotatory savings circles (Ajo/Esusu) with automated payout tracking.
* Airtime & Utility Payments: Purchase airtime and pay bills directly from the wallet.
* Crypto Integration: Support for Web3-based payments and wallet addresses.

5. PRIVACY & ELITE SECURITY
---------------------------
* Screenshot Prevention: Blocks screenshots and screen recordings in private chats.
* Biometric App Lock: Secure the app using Fingerprint or Face ID via Credential Manager.
* Stealth Mode: Hide "Online" status and read receipts while browsing.
* Disappearing Messages: Automated self-destruct timers (24h, 7 days, 90 days).
* Emergency SOS: One-tap button to share live location and record 30s of emergency audio.
* Locked Chats: Use biometrics to hide and secure specific sensitive conversations.

6. PERSONALIZATION & UI
-----------------------
* Titan Elite Status: Exclusive animated profile badges and priority support for verified users.
* Advanced Themes: Light, Dark, and specialized OLED Black mode.
* Bubble Roundness: Customizable message bubble shape (0px to 30px).
* Chat Wallpapers: Cloudinary-hosted background library for chat threads.
* Global Font Customization: Adjustable font sizes and multiple premium typefaces.
* Icon-Only Navigation: Clean, modern bottom navigation bar without text clutter.
* Glassmorphism UI: Optional blur effects for AppBars and navigation elements.

TECHNICAL SPECS
---------------
* Platform: Flutter (Cross-platform)
* Backend: Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions)
* Media: Cloudinary AI & Storage
* Target Android API: 36 (Android 15)
* Minimum Android Version: 5.0 (API 21)
