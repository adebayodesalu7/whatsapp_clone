TITAN CHATAPP - SUPER-APP EVOLUTION ROADMAP

Building the future of Nigerian Social Commerce and Savings.

PROPOSED UPGRADES:

1. TITAN AI VENDOR ASSISTANT (Marketplace Upgrade)
   - AI Catalog Generator: Upload a product photo, and Titan AI generates a professional description, suggests a category, and identifies competitive pricing based on marketplace trends.
   - AI Auto-Responder: Vendors can enable a "Business Bot" that handles inquiries (price, availability, size) automatically based on their active catalog.

2. LOGISTICS & REAL-TIME TRACKING (Marketplace Upgrade)
   - Titan Delivery Integration: Connect with local dispatch services (Gokada, Kwik, etc.).
   - Live Tracking: Buyers can track their marketplace orders on a real-time map within the chat.
   - Proof of Delivery: Secure QR-code handover system between dispatch rider and buyer to release Escrow funds.

3. ADVANCED UTILITY HUB (Wallet Upgrade)
   - Bill Payments: Beyond airtime, add Electricity (IKEDC/EKEDC), Cable TV (DSTV/GOTV), and Data bundles.
   - Scan to Pay: QR code payment system for physical vendor stores using Titan Wallet.
   - Micro-Loans: Social-backed peer-to-peer lending within verified Ajo groups (interest-free loans from the community pot).

4. TITAN MOMENTS & SHOPPABLE STATUS (Social Upgrade)
   - E-commerce Integration in Status: Users can tag their marketplace items in status updates. Viewers can click "Buy Now" directly from the Status/Story.
   - Verified Business Profiles: Professional portfolios for vendors showing their "Trust Score", successful deliveries, and community reviews.

5. PRIVACY & SECURITY ENHANCEMENTS (Core Upgrade)
   - Stealth Mode: Per-contact privacy settings to hide "Online" status and "Blue Ticks" for specific users while keeping them for others.
   - Encrypted Local Backup: Secure chat and transaction backup to personal Google Drive or local storage.
   - Biometric Payment Confirmation: Require face/fingerprint ID for every wallet transaction above ₦5,000.

6. COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE (Ajo Upgrade)
   - Group Voting: Use the Polls feature to formally vote on Ajo rule changes (e.g., increasing contribution amount or skipping a turn).
   - Social Guarantee: Members can "vouch" for new joiners in public Ajo groups, linking their own Trust Score to the new member's performance.
